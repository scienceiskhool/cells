# Microscope Lab

A light-microscope simulation for Science is Khool.

- **G1 Science (Ch 9 Cells):** identify microscope parts, focus on human cheek cells, then type the labels (cell membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus).
- **G2/G3 Science (Ch 6 Model of Cells):** the same, with cheek cells, onion epidermis and pondweed leaf, plus the plant-cell labels.
- Finishing all slides earns a **Pre-Lab badge** (name, class, register no., date) shown on the start screen. It is saved in the student's browser.
- The badge unlocks **Challenge mode**: 5 mystery slides against the clock (cheek, onion, pondweed, blood smear, root hair). G2/G3 also identify animal or plant cell.

Open `microscope.html`.

## Files

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `microscope.html` | The whole sim |
| `challenge.mp3` | Challenge-mode music (looped) |
| `favicon.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` | Tab and home-screen icons |

Settings you can change are near the top of the script: `MUSIC_FILE`, `MUSIC_VOLUME`.
